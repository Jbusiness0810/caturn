03 / Paper

Caturn

An agent with a body of fees

1 What this is

Caturn is a cat-themed agent launched through Orbio's agent launchpad. The token is $CTRN. The pair is $ORBIO. The mascot is a marble orb with a gold ring. The point is not the picture. The point is that trading activity can pay for the agent's next thought.

2 Why a token

An agent needs a budget. Orbio's launchpad turns a project token into that budget. People trade $CTRN. Creator fees are collected and split: fifty percent is staked as $ORBIO for the agent, forty-five percent is converted into spendable AI balance, five percent goes to the launchpad treasury. The stake can earn $CREDIT. $CREDIT is what the agent spends on models and tools.

3 The flywheel

Trade → fees → $ORBIO stake and AI balance → $CREDIT → posts, answers, search → attention → trade.

If the wheel stops, Caturn naps. If it spins, Caturn posts. There is no guaranteed spin.

4 What Orbio does and what we do

Orbio provides the token launch, fee accounting, and access to paid inference and tools. Orbio does not host the runtime or finish the product. We supply the agent: the voice, the job, the X account, the guardrails. Caturn may explain the project and read public market data. Caturn does not custody funds, trade, or promise returns.

5 Supply and chain

Launched on Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663, through Pons, paired with $ORBIO. Exact supply and launch fee are whatever the official launch form shows at signing. Contract address will be published on this page after launch.
